# Chelsea Luther Brand Guide

## Core idea
Chelsea helps people navigate high-stakes conversations with clarity, confidence, and intention.

## Brand character
- Editorial
- Warm
- Thoughtful
- Direct
- Human
- Quietly confident
- Practical

## Design principles
- Typography first
- Generous whitespace
- Photography over graphics
- Calm, restrained motion
- Magazine rhythm rather than a corporate grid
- Layouts must remain flexible enough to accept new photography later

## Writing principles
- Lead with understanding, not expertise
- Be specific without becoming clinical
- Make complex ideas feel usable
- Sound grounded, never performative

## Preferred language
- High-stakes conversations
- Self-advocacy
- Communication
- Leadership
- Influence
- Clarity
- Intention
- Practical behavior change

## Avoid
- Negotiation
- Transform your life
- Unlock your potential
- Crush your goals
- Generic motivational language
- Corporate jargon
- Coaching clichés

## Audience distinction
Organizations buy certainty. Individuals buy hope.

The two audiences should remain visually connected but emotionally distinct.
