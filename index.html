My approach

Warm enough to feel human. Direct enough to create movement.

Curiosity before certainty. People before strategies. Practical over performative.

My work begins with precise questions and genuine curiosity about what is happening beneath the surface. I believe people create lasting change when they understand themselves more clearly—not when they are handed another script to follow.

My background spans life coaching, sales leadership, executive facilitation, and building and leading global teams. That combination allows me to bring emotional intelligence and practical structure into the same room.

Whether I am coaching one person or speaking to a room of leaders, the goal is the same: people leave not just inspired, but equipped.
