About Chelsea

Clear thinking. Precise questions. Forward momentum.

As a Certified Life Coach, I’ve supported hundreds of clients navigating high-emotion dynamics, competing priorities, and internal barriers to effective action.

I’m known for asking precise questions to quickly identify what’s really driving a situation, then helping people move forward with clarity and intention.

In the corporate space, I’ve built and led high-performing sales teams globally. Today, I work with leaders and organizations to navigate difficult conversations, influence behavior, and translate strategy into clear next steps.

Whether I’m coaching one person or speaking to a room of executives, my goal is the same: people leave not just inspired, but equipped.
