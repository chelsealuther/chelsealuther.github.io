My approach

Warm enough to feel human.
Direct enough to create movement.

01

Curiosity before certainty.

The most useful conversations begin by getting underneath the first answer. I ask precise questions, listen for what is not yet being said, and help people see the situation more clearly.

02

People before strategies.

Tools matter, but they only work when they reflect the person using them. My background in coaching, sales leadership, and executive facilitation lets me bring emotional intelligence and practical structure into the same room.

03

Practical over performative.

The goal is never to leave people briefly inspired. It is to help them leave equipped—with language, perspective, and a next step they can actually use.
