About

As a Certified Life Coach, I’ve supported hundreds of clients navigating high-emotion dynamics, competing priorities, and internal barriers to effective action. I’m known for asking precise questions to quickly identify what’s really driving a situation, and helping people move forward with clarity and intention.

In the corporate space, I’ve built and led high-performing sales teams globally, and I thrive in fast-paced, results-driven environments. I’m especially passionate about helping leaders and teams navigate difficult conversations, influence behavior, and translate strategy into clear next steps.

As a speaker and facilitator, I engage diverse audiences by simplifying complex ideas and delivering actionable guidance. My style is direct, grounded, and focused on creating forward momentum—so people leave not just inspired, but equipped.
